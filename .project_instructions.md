## Gift Animation System (Updated May 2026)
- `components/gift-animation-overlay.tsx` - Spectacular full-screen gift animation overlay
  - Particle system (emoji + colored particles) varying by gift price tier
  - Full-screen flash for expensive gifts (200+), luxury spotlight for 500+
  - Combo counter (x2, x3, etc.) for repeated gifts from same sender
  - Side entry banner for smaller gifts, center stage for expensive
  - Queue-based: processes one gift animation at a time
  - Used by both viewer page and broadcaster page via `onTrigger` callback ref pattern
- Price tiers: normal (1-49), mid (50-149), expensive (200-499), luxury (500+)
